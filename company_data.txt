ADVERTISING CREDITS SYSTEM

Instantlly Cards runs on a credit-based advertising model. Credits are the digital currency of the platform used to power all paid promotions.

How it works:
1 Credit equals 1 Rupee in advertising value. Credits are purchased through one-time investment plans and never expire. Users can spend credits on featured business listings, promoted classified ads, event sponsorship and highlights, banner advertisements, top search rankings, and voucher promotions.

The key benefit is that users receive far more credits than the amount they invest. For example, investing Rs 15,000 gives Rs 1,00,000 worth of advertising credits — a 6 to 7 times return on investment. The difference between what is paid and the credits received is described by the company as a marketing commission that the user can use to grow their business.

FRANCHISE AND PARTNER PROGRAM

Instantlly Cards offers two partnership models for individuals or businesses who want to earn by growing the platform.

City Franchise Partner
This is the premium partnership model where a partner owns exclusive rights for an entire city. The revenue share is 30 to 40 percent on all transactions within the city. Partners can onboard businesses and users, manage local events and promotions, and access marketing materials and campaigns. A dedicated support team is assigned. Investment ranges from Rs 2 to 5 lakhs depending on the city.

Channel Partner
This is a lighter model with no territorial restrictions. Partners earn 20 to 25 percent commission on referrals and can work from anywhere in India. Minimal investment is required between Rs 10,000 and Rs 50,000. Comprehensive sales training, marketing support, and materials are provided.

Both programs offer multiple income streams including subscriptions, events, classifieds, and vouchers. Instantlly provides complete training, technical support, marketing assets, and an operational support system for all partners.
